How does Free System Maintenance work?

Your maintenance days are completely flexible — you do not have to use them all at once or on consecutive days.

Example — Standard Package (2 Weeks):
Use 3 days this week for a content update. Use 2 more days next month for new photos. The remaining days stay available until your full 2 weeks are used up.
Important — Revisions and Delivery Time:  Each revision round adds extra days to your expected delivery date. To keep things moving efficiently, please send all change requests together in a single message per revision round.

Domain and hosting are sold separately — approximately P500–P1,500/year for domain and P3,000–P8,000/year for hosting. Full setup guidance is included.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is domain and hosting included in the package? ▼
Not included, but very affordable. Domain runs around P500–P1,500 per year and hosting around P3,000–P8,000 per year. I will walk you through purchasing and setting everything up.
Can I update my website after delivery? ▼
Yes. WordPress websites are easy to manage even without technical knowledge — you can update text, photos, and listings on your own. I'll walk you through the basics after delivery, and the free maintenance is there whenever you need an extra hand.
What platform will you use to build my website? ▼
WordPress for Standard and Premium packages — the most flexible and widely-used website platform available. GitHub Pages for simpler portfolio sites. I will recommend the right fit based on your specific requirements.
How exactly does the free maintenance work? ▼
The maintenance days are flexible and do not expire quickly. You can use them any time — spread across different weeks or months — until the total days are fully used up. Just send a message whenever you need assistance and we'll sort it out.
Do revisions affect the delivery date? ▼
Yes. Every revision round requested adds extra days to the expected delivery date. To keep the timeline as short as possible, please collect all your change requests and send them in one message per revision round.
Will I fully own the website after delivery? ▼
Yes, completely. Once the website is approved and delivered, you receive full ownership — all access credentials, all files. No ongoing fees to me. Your only recurring costs are your domain and hosting renewals.
